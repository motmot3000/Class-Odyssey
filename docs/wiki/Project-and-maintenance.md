# Project and maintenance

## An independent take on classroom gamification

Class Odyssey is a personal, open-source project. It was built to be genuinely
usable in a classroom: the board must stay readable when projected, actions must
be fast during the lesson, and progress must be understandable at a glance.

Its design is **deliberately inspired by Classcraft**, which remains far more
developed: a mature product with more features, more classroom history and a
full team behind it. Class Odyssey explores the same core idea — a persistent
hero per student, XP and levels, HP, and a teacher console for rewards and
sanctions — in a smaller, self-hostable form. It is not a competitor, a
replacement or an equivalent.

The project is still young. Accounts, progression, backups, the shop, 3D,
translations and administration already exist, but not everything is stable.
Old experiments and prototypes remain next to the active runtime.

## Transparency about AI

Part of the development was **vibe coded** with AI tools. The project does not
claim to have been entirely written by hand or audited line by line. It does ask
contributors to read, understand and verify changes, with tests and evidence
adapted to each change.

## Maintenance commitment

The project is maintained by **[@motmot3000](https://github.com/motmot3000)**,
who follows it over time and gradually consolidates the code, dependencies and
documentation to preserve usability and longevity. This work moves **as time
allows**.

There is no professional support contract, no permanent monitoring and no
promise of a response or fix within a given delay. Known issues and priorities
can change with classroom feedback.

## How to help

Contributors are welcome: code, documentation, tests, accessibility,
translations, field reports and original graphic work. A first conversation
helps choose a useful and reasonable change.

The [roadmap](https://github.com/motmot3000/Class-Odyssey/blob/main/docs/project-status.md)
separates the existing foundation from priorities, without promised dates.
